## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Released-version fixture corpus after format freeze
From the release that freezes format v1 for general availability, every released minor version SHALL contribute a fixture corpus: a small set of sealed HEF files, HEJ segments, and footer-mirror objects actually written by that release's writer, committed to the repository together with a generator that asserts the writer version it ran under. Every future reader SHALL read the entire corpus in CI — opening, validating checksums, scanning, and aggregating each fixture — and a reader change that fails any released-version fixture SHALL NOT ship. The corpus SHALL grow monotonically: a fixture is added when its version releases and SHALL NOT be regenerated by a newer writer, because its value is precisely that its bytes came from the released code. Before the freeze, deterministic simulation and fuzzing remain the compatibility story; this requirement adds the released-bytes discipline at the point the format stops moving.

#### Scenario: A new reader proves it reads every released version
- **WHEN** a reader change lands after GA
- **THEN** CI reads every committed fixture from every released minor version — open, verify, scan, aggregate — and the change ships only if all pass

#### Scenario: Fixtures are never regenerated
- **WHEN** a newer writer could produce a fixture's content differently
- **THEN** the committed fixture keeps the bytes its released writer produced, and only a new fixture for the new version is added

#### Scenario: A compatibility break is caught before shipping
- **WHEN** a reader change makes an old fixture unreadable or changes its scanned rows
- **THEN** the fixture gate fails and the change does not ship until the incompatibility is resolved
